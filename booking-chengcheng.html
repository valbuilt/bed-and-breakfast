Description

Family-run Bed & Breakfast, surrounded by nature but within walking distance of the town center. Eco-friendly, great for couples or friends, and perfect base for all outdoor sports and visits to the nearby Arte Sella or the lakes of Levico and Caldonazzo. Bicycle rental is available in the nearby village of Borgo Valsugana.

Hosted by Sara
